# LifegroupLeaders.com — Complete Study (Antioch Waco Lifegroup Model)

> Research compiled 2026-07-16 from lifegroupleaders.com (all 30+ toolbox resources), discipleshiplessons.com, and the Discipleship Roadmap PDF. This is the source-of-truth document for designing the Lifegroup Mega Tool.

---

## 1. The DNA: Acts 2:42–47

The entire model is an exegesis of Acts 2:42–47. Each phrase becomes a named value. The seven values (canonical list from the Vision Study Guide):

1. **Devotion to Scripture** — "loving God's Word, listening to God's Word, living out God's Word, leading others according to God's Word"
2. **Devotion to Fellowship & Breaking Bread** — "loving, authentic, joyful, intentional Christ-centered communities"
3. **Devotion to Prayer** — prayer as "part of the fabric" personally and collectively
4. **God's Presence & Power at Work** — awe, signs and wonders, testimonies, spiritual gifts (1 Cor 14:26)
5. **The Lost Being Saved and Added** — "You're not a Bible study that occasionally invites people. You're a Spirit-filled community on mission with Jesus to seek and save the lost."
6. **The 5 Circles** (house to house AND in the temple courts) — integration into the broader church
7. **Christlike Leaders & Leadership Teams** — abiding, character, team-based ministry

**Every value page follows the same template** (this IS their health framework):
Q → What This Value Is → Why It Matters → How Do I Help My Group Live This Out? (numbered practices, always starting with "Lead by example: Jesus is our model") → **Next Steps: "What is the Holy Spirit highlighting as you and your group's next step in growing in this value?"**

Tagline: **"Plant, lead and multiply house to house community in your sphere of influence."**

## 2. The 5 Circles (per-disciple health model)

Vision: "each disciple would be part of the 5 Circles of church and each group would be integrated into our broader church family."

| Circle | Scale | Definition | Jesus's model |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Alone | Time alone with the Father (devotional life) | Luke 5:16 |
| 2 | Pair/few | Life-on-life discipleship | Matt 4:19 |
| 3 | Household | Sharing life house to house (the Lifegroup) | Luke 22:14–15 |
| 4 | Congregation | Gathering with the people of God (Sundays) | Luke 4:16 |
| 5 | World | Living on mission as a witness | Luke 19:10 |

A **disciple** is healthy when engaged in all five circles. A **group** is healthy when integrated (not isolated) under joyful oversight.

## 3. Organizational hierarchy (data model backbone)

**Church (elders/pastors) → Zones (Zone Pastor) → Sections (Section Leader) → Lifegroups (leader + co-leaders + interns) → Discipleship Groups / mentoring pairs → Disciples**

- Every Lifegroup leader should be connected to both a Section Leader and a Zone Pastor.
- Lifegroup leadership team: co-ed (≥1 male + ≥1 female leader), plus interns, plus a worship leader.
- Section Leader approves plants; new-leader details reported to Zone Pastor post-plant.
- Named roles inside a group: worship leader, discussion facilitator, greeter, prayer recorder, refreshment rotation, children's-area volunteer, "connector" (assigned to newcomers).

## 4. Discipleship structure (tree model)

From "Organizing Discipleship Groups through Lifegroup" + discipleshiplessons.com:

- **Definition:** "intentional relationships focused on growing as lifelong, obedient followers of Jesus who in turn help others to do the same."
- **Two formats:**
  - **Peer-to-peer:** 3–5 people, same gender, similar maturity ("sharpen one another")
  - **Mentoring:** more mature believer → newer believer (the asymmetric tree edge)
- **Gender segregation mandatory** in discipleship groups (lifegroups are mixed).
- **Cohort kickoffs: October, February, June** each year.
- **Health target:** "A strong Lifegroup will have at least two men's discipleship groups and two women's discipleship groups" — D-group density (2+2) is explicitly the readiness marker for planting.
- Vision: "everyone would both be a disciple and be a part of making disciples" — every node should have both an upstream and downstream edge.
- **Who to disciple (4 criteria):** hungry, humble/teachable, faithful/reliable (2 Tim 2:2), prayerfully discerned.

### Discipleship Roadmap (per-person stage ladder, from discipleshiplessons.com)
1. **Start Here:** Discipleship DTR → Share Your Stories
2. **Seekers:** Gospel of John Bible Study
3. **Foundations for New Believers (6):** Gospel Presentation → Sharing Your Testimony → Water Baptism → Deliverance Prayer → Baptism of the Holy Spirit → Church Membership
4. **Growing Disciples — Look Up** (relationship with God): devotional life, Word, worship, prayer, listening, abiding (+ 40 Days of Devotion)
5. **Look In** (inner transformation): new identity, confession, repentance, replacement, restoration (+ Freedom Prayer Experience)
6. **Look Out** (mission): witness identity, sphere of influence, sharing the Gospel, making disciples who make disciples → **"Help Them Find Their First Disciple"** (the node becomes a parent)
7. **What's Next:** Discipleship Planning Guide, Going Deeper menus (incl. Wesley's 22 questions)

The Roadmap PDF is a literal checkbox checklist — each lesson is a checkable milestone. Dated salvation decisions are recordable ("On __/__/____ I received Jesus").

## 5. Group lifecycle: Seasons of a Lifegroup

Circular three-season lifecycle:

1. **Starting Up / Planting / Replanting** — new or restarted groups: establish core team, review values, unify vision, build relationships, identify outreach targets. (New plants run the 4-Week Start Plan.)
2. **Building Up** — healthy growing groups: vision casting, **develop discipleship relationships (key at this stage)**, outreach focus, follow up Sunday guests, evangelism strategy.
3. **Planting / Multiplying** — identify emerging leaders (those already making disciples), shared multiplication vision, set plant date, train leaders, plant and **celebrate**.

Also: **replanting** (restart of a stagnant group) and **honorable transitions** (leader steps back for a season) are named events.

## 6. Planting Readiness Assessment (the 15-point health rubric)

Gate questions: Enough leaders? Enough people (≥8 core members per new group; family groups ≥10 kids)? What is God saying?

- **Attendance (max 4):** 1 pt per 3 core members attending 3–4×/month (excluding leadership team)
- **Leadership (max 6, 1 pt each):** leaders/interns attend consistently; gender balance per new group; both teams have facilitated ≥3 meetings; all feel called; all completed intern process; interns identified for the new groups
- **Readiness (max 5, 1 pt each):** meeting locations set; leaders motivated & vision-communicating; teams in group ≥2 months & bonded; leadership seen as privilege not obligation; worship leader ready for both groups

**12–15 → prepare to plant. ≤11 → not yet.**

## 7. Multiplication process (5-week countdown)

Prereqs: Section Leader approval + group buy-in.
- **T-5 wks:** announce plant date, begin vision casting ("we are called to multiply the life of God")
- **T-4/3 wks:** build momentum, emerging leaders practice facilitating, address concerns
- **T-2 wks:** announce new leadership teams; members pray about which group to join
- **T-1 wk:** intensify vision; collect **written commitments** on group preference; balance the groups
- **Plant night:** party, celebrate, separate prayer gatherings, photos
- **Post-launch:** report new leaders to Zone Pastor; monthly support; new group runs the **4-Week Start Plan** (Vision & Expectations → Community & Purpose → Eating Together → Heart Connections)

## 8. Leadership pipeline

**Stages: member → emerging leader → apprentice/intern → leader.**

- **8 qualities of a potential leader:** love for Jesus & His people, faithfulness, humility, heart to serve, vision alignment, leadership potential, teachable character, **already a disciplemaker**
- **Selection:** prayer → section leader input → gradual testing → direct conversation
- **8-week apprenticeship:** Wks 1–3 foundation (shadowing, small tasks) → Wks 4–5 skill development (leads vision/prayer, debriefs) → Wks 6–8 independent leadership
- **Training model: Watch → Explain → Do → Coach**
- **Leader standard (4 Cs):** Calling (born again, member of the church, called to make disciples), Character (fruit of the Spirit), Chemistry (humility with co-leaders and church leadership), Commitments (zone meetings, leading Acts 2:42–47 community, making disciples)

## 9. Meeting model

**Segment vocabulary:** Welcome, Vision, Announcements, Connection Question, Testimony, Worship, Discussion, Prayer/Ministry Time.

**Four classic formats:**
1. Weekly 90 min + separate discipleship meetings
2. Weekly 120 min (75 all together + 45 discipleship breakouts: Look Up/In/Out)
3. Bi-weekly alternating (full gathering wks 1&3; discipleship wks 2&4)
4. Weekly 90 min family-inclusive (45 with kids + 45 adults while kids play)

Formats 1–2 for young adults/no kids; 3–4 for families. Chosen in consultation with Zone Pastor/Section Leader.

**Pre-meeting:** 60-min leadership team meeting 1 hr before group: check-in (8) → worship (12) → member-by-member updates & discipleship check (12) → prayer for members by name (15) → tonight's plan & role assignments (8) → admin (2).

**Prayer ministry rules:** break into 2–3s (consider gender-separated); **"always end with prayer for the lost" at every meeting**; document requests in writing; revisit previous requests.

**Kids (5 C's):** Come Together → Celebrate → Contribute → Connect → Come On, Let 'Em Play. Optional monthly rotation (all-ages / women's extended / creative / men's extended).

## 10. Trackable systems named on the site

- **Prayer Board System:** physical or digital board; designated recorder; specific requests (3 min/person cap); weekly focus; answers documented beside requests over time; reviewed in sessions; displayed prominently; prayer journal compiles requests + answers. Pillars: request tracking, answer documentation, weekly focus, group participation. Answered prayers feed the Testimony segment.
- **Testimony taxonomy:** prayer answers, salvations, healings (physical/emotional/relational), provision, guidance, character growth, breakthrough.
- **Oikos Evangelism:** each member's oikos = 8–15 people (household/relational world). Process: Awareness (list lost people by name) → Training (bridge diagram, 2-minute testimony, prayer for the sick) → Involvement & Accountability (share oikos requests, **pray for lost friends by name in meetings**, celebrate progress).
- **4-week newcomer follow-up:** Wk1 leader personally calls/texts day after visit → Wk2 assign a "connector" → Wk3 shared meal outside group → Wk4 assess, address barriers, invite to serve, connect to Sundays.
- **HELP pastoral care:** Have patience → Equip with the Word → Lead to God's presence → Point to other resources (escalate to Zone Pastor for referral when beyond leader's expertise).
- **Conflict resolution:** Antioch Waco Conflict Resolution Policy (2022) PDF.
- **Leader prayer rhythm:** daily (member list) / weekly (before + at group) / monthly (half-nights, prayer walks, fasting, retreats).
- **This Week in Lifegroups:** weekly Sunday email tying groups to the sermon series, with discussion guides — a weekly content-drop cadence.

## 11. Culture & tone (critical for product design)

- Spirit-led, invitational, celebration-heavy — NOT metric-driven. Every guide ends with "What is the Holy Spirit highlighting as your next step in growing in this value?"
- Multiplication is a party, not a split. Plant nights get photos and celebration.
- "Lead by example: Jesus is our model" opens every practice list.
- Language matters: Lifegroup (one word), house to house, spiritual family, the lost, oikos, sphere of influence, cast vision, flock, plant/replant, emerging leader, intern, one-on-ones, Look Up/Look In/Look Out, ministry time, breakthrough, Next Steps.
- Frame indicators as **"next steps in growing"** rather than scores/grades. Health language should invite discernment ("what is the Holy Spirit highlighting?"), not judgment.

## 12. Implied health dimensions (for a health-tracking feature)

Per-group, the 7 values as dimensions with observable practices:
1. Scripture: sermon guides used, Bibles present, Word applied in D-groups
2. Fellowship: meal/social cadence, milestones celebrated, one-another acts, attendance consistency
3. Prayer: prayer board active, requests→answers tracked, prayer nights
4. Presence & Power: testimonies logged (by category), gifts practiced, worship
5. Lost Saved & Added: oikos lists maintained, members trained, outreach events, salvations, newcomers in follow-up pipeline
6. 5 Circles: members engaged across all five circles
7. Leadership: leadership team complete (co-ed + worship leader), interns in pipeline, connected to Section Leader/Zone Pastor, D-group density vs. 2+2 target

Plus the hard rubric: the 15-point Planting Readiness Assessment (threshold 12).
